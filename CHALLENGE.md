## The Gaming Challenge

Lewis's audience is made up largely of male gamers in their mid-20s to mid-30s with a genuine passion for gaming, TV, and film. They discover games everywhere, but there is no good place to save the ones they mean to play. Something spotted on TikTok, YouTube, or Instagram is often gone by the time they remember it.

Build a gaming bucket list where people can:

- Add games
- Mark games as complete
- Rate games
- Share lists with friends

## What Judges Are Looking For

Judges are looking for a solution that:

- Makes it effortless to save a game in the moment of discovery
- Keeps a backlog useful and enjoyable instead of turning it into another chore
- Supports completing and rating games
- Makes sharing and comparing lists with friends feel natural
- Reflects the energy and enthusiasm of people who genuinely love games and gaming culture
